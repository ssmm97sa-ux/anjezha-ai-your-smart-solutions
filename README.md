# Anjezha AI: Your Smart Solutions

Build a professional Arabic RTL website called "أنجزها AI" (Anjezha AI), targeting customers in the UAE and Arab world.

The website provides simple AI-powered digital services.

Main services:

1. Professional CV creation

2. Professional email and letter writing

3. Document summarization

4. Arabic and English rewriting

5. Content writing

6. Translation

Design:

- Arabic-first and fully RTL

- Modern, premium and trustworthy

- Simple and very easy to use on mobile

- White and dark navy design with subtle blue/purple AI accents

- Brand name: أنجزها AI

- Currency: AED

Homepage:

- Hero headline: "أنجزها بالذكاء الاصطناعي"

- Subtitle: "خدمات رقمية سريعة واحترافية تساعدك تنجز شغلك بسهولة"

- Button: "ابدأ الآن"

Add a services section with clear cards and starting prices.

Suggested starting prices:

CV creation: 49 AED

Professional email/letter: 19 AED

Document summarization: 19 AED

Rewriting: 15 AED

Content writing: 29 AED

Translation: 19 AED

For each service add a button: "اطلب الخدمة"

Create an order page where the customer can:

- Choose a service

- Enter their name

- Enter email

- Enter WhatsApp/mobile number

- Describe what they need

- Upload a file if needed

- See the price

- Click a payment button

Also create:

- About section

- FAQ

- Contact section

- Privacy policy

- Terms and conditions

Do not integrate Stripe or any paid payment provider yet.

For now create the payment button and payment step as placeholders so we can connect Ziina later.

Make the website feel like a real UAE-based Arabic AI services business, not a generic template.
